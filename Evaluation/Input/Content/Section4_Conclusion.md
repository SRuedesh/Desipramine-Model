The desipramine PBPK model describes the evaluated plasma concentration-time data for desipramine and 2-hydroxydesipramine after intravenous administration of 50 mg and oral doses of 25-100 mg in adults. The evaluation includes CYP2D6 poor, extensive, normal, and higher-activity groups represented by phenotype or activity score.

The model uses common absorption and distribution parameters across CYP2D6 groups. CYP2D6 forms 2-hydroxydesipramine, while residual hepatic clearance and passive glomerular filtration contribute to parent and metabolite elimination. CYP2D6 characterization-specific catalytic rates describe the observed exposure differences across the evaluated groups.

The concentration-time profiles and goodness-of-fit diagnostics characterize model performance within the evaluated dose, route, analyte, and CYP2D6 ranges. No formal acceptance criterion was applied. This compound report evaluates only desipramine-only or control study arms.

The main limitations are unresolved genotype differences within activity scores, empirical residual clearance, and limited metabolite data.
